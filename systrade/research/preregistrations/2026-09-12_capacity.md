# Pre-registration — what the paper books become at real capital

Written 2026-09-12, after the fill-cost measurement (LEDGER row 35) and a
snapshot of today's target books at Rs 30 lakh (sizes only, no returns), and
before any rupee-level simulation has been run.

## 1. The question

Every paper track is a book of WEIGHTS: fractional shares, a full rebalance
every month, a flat 50 bps round trip. A real account holds whole shares,
pays Rs 14.75 on every sale however small, obeys Law 12's position inertia,
and at Rs 30 lakh gives the combination track ~490 positions of ~Rs 6,000.
Does the rupee book still reproduce the weight book the evidence is about —
and at what capital, under which minimum-position rule?

This is a MEASUREMENT of implementation fidelity, not a test of an edge:
**trials=0**. No return against a control is computed or consulted. The
deciding statistics compare the rupee book with its own frozen weight book.

## 2. Configurations (fixed; 20 cells)

- **Tracks:** the five frozen specs as registered in `internal/paper`
  (trend-quintile, trend-speed-blend, momentum-lookback-blend,
  low-volatility-blend, momentum-lowvol-combination). Selection untouched.
- **Capital:** Rs 30 lakh and Rs 1 crore, held constant: every rebalance
  sizes targets off the nominal capital, not the running NAV, so the answer
  is "what this much money holds", not a compounding path.
- **Minimum position:** none, or **Rs 10,000** — every name in the book gets
  at least Rs 10,000, and the total deployed is allowed to exceed the capital
  (the operator's rule, 2026-09-12).

## 3. Mechanics (fixed; the same in every cell)

- Targets are the spec's own weights × capital (floored at the minimum when
  one applies), converted to whole shares at the day's RAW opening price
  (adjusted open × close/adj_close — checked against the bhavcopy on
  2019-03-01/05 for a 1:10 split, a bonus and an unadjusted name), rounded to
  the nearest share. A name whose nearest whole number is zero is not bought.
- **Law 12, position inertia:** a held name is traded only when its share
  count is more than 10% away from its target; entries and exits always
  trade. Carver's rule, not a parameter.
- Fills at the opening auction price (the open), as every track assumes.
- Costs per trade from `internal/costs` (Dhan's schedule, checked against
  dhan.co/pricing on 2026-09-12): statutory charges per side, Rs 14.75 per
  sale, and square-root impact with Y = 1 on the name's 60-day median traded
  value and daily volatility. No spread (auction fills).
- Returns are on the rupees actually deployed; cash is reported separately.
- Decisions 2013-07-01 → 2021-12-31, the exploration years every screen
  used. Nothing from 2022+ is read. Rupees are nominal: 2013's universe is
  smaller, so early positions are larger than today's — which is why the
  snapshot of today's books is reported beside the simulation.

## 4. What is reported, per cell

Names held, median and 10th-percentile position, deployed capital against
nominal, cost per year (statutory / DP / impact, and total against the
50-bps model the evidence assumed), turnover, the share of position-days
holding fewer than 2 shares (Law 14: a maximum position of 2× the average
must be at least 4 blocks), names targeted but rounded to zero, and the
tracking error and mean difference of the rupee book's gross daily return
against the weight book's.

## 5. Decision rule — a cell is FIT to carry the paper evidence when all hold

- **F1:** tracking error of gross daily returns against the weight book
  ≤ 2% a year. (The evidence is edges of 6-10% a year at 14-22% volatility;
  2% of noise does not swamp it.)
- **F2:** realised cost per year ≤ the 50-bps model's cost per year for the
  same track — the cost every result in the ledger already paid.
- **F3:** fewer than 5% of position-days hold under 2 shares (Law 14).

The recommendation is the smallest capital at which a track is FIT, and the
plain book (no minimum) over the Rs 10,000 floor where both are FIT, because
the floor changes the spec's weights and the plain book does not. A FIT cell
still starts a new forward paper record at that capital before money moves
(RESEARCH_PROTOCOL: forward paper trading is the gate).

## 6. Ledger

LEDGER row 36, MEASUREMENT, trials=0, whatever the outcome. Command:
`paper capacity -strategy <name> -from 2013-07-01 -to 2021-12-31`.

## 7. Amendment — written after trend-quintile's output, before any other track's

The Rs 10 crore universe was far smaller in the exploration years: the
trend-quintile book held a median of 24-80 names a year in 2013-2021 against
160 today, so at Rs 30 lakh its historical positions were Rs 35,000-124,000,
not today's Rs 18,750. The nominal run passes F1-F3 at sizes that no longer
exist, and cannot answer the question.

Added, as a second run of every cell, F1-F3 unchanged: **today-sized.** At
each rebalance the capital is scaled by (names in that day's book ÷ names in
today's book), so every historical position is the size today's book gives
it. Today's books, from the 2026-09-12 order sheets: trend-quintile 160,
trend-speed-blend 160, momentum-lookback-blend 241, low-volatility-blend 279,
momentum-lowvol-combination 493. The nominal run stays in the report; the
recommendation reads the today-sized run. What was seen before this amendment:
trend-quintile's nominal cells only (all four FIT), and no return of any book
against a control.
