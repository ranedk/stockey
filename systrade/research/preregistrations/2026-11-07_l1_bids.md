# Pre-registration — "lowest bidder" (L1) announcements as a short trade

Written 2026-11-07, before any L1 announcement for it was fetched or any return around one was
computed. Operator, after rows 84-88: "if these companies get a jump on the announcement day or
post that, and if the 5-10-15 day price is even 2-3% higher, then that's a good short trade";
then: "test the lowest bidder stage".

**Prior evidence, stated now:**
- Rows 84-88 (order wins, 2018-22): the move is in the 20 sessions before the filing and on its
  day. Every entry after the news lost over 1-15 sessions, even one minute after the filing.
- The research report (`reports/Order wins and results signals.md`) says practitioners buy the
  L1 stage, which comes weeks before the award. Ashoka Buildcon, Ceigall and RVNL were cited.
- The 2018-22 order candidates hold only 41 texts that mention L1. The order prefilter needed
  words like "order" or "contract", so most L1 filings were never kept. Their returns were
  never computed.

**Story (Law 1):** declaring a company L1 means it is very likely to win the contract, but the
award, and often the value disclosure, comes weeks later. If investors wait for the award, the
price may rise between L1 and award, and that rise can be caught by buying after the L1 news.

## 1. Data

- Universe: the same 1,788 NSE EQ symbols as row 84 (`orders.universe()`), the same sector
  exclusions, and the same 60-session median traded value >= Rs 5 cr at s0.
- Announcements: every company's NSE announcement list for 2018-01-01 to 2025-12-31, refetched
  through the NSE gate. A filing is kept if its text or category matches the L1 prefilter:
  "L1" / "L-1" / "L 1" as a word, or "lowest bid / bidder / tender / quote / offer / price",
  or "lowest evaluated". Rows go to new tables, not the row 84 ones.
- Classification is outcome-blind and runs before any return is computed. Claude, the tagger's
  model, decides for each kept filing whether the company ITSELF, or a JV it leads, has been
  declared L1 / lowest bidder for a NEW contract that is not yet awarded.
  - Not counted: an actual award (that is row 84's event); a repeat mention; a bid that was
    merely submitted; L1 status of a subsidiary unless the text calls it the group's; "L1" in
    another sense.
  - Value, unit, currency and the verbatim sentence are checked in code and converted to
    Rs crore, exactly as in row 84.
  - Missing values: if fewer than 70% of L1 events have a stated value in either period, the
    attached PDF is read for those without a value. This is row 84's rule: text layer only,
    first 3 pages, the same for both periods.
- Revenue, prices and industries: as row 84.

## 2. Event and timing

- **Event:** an L1 announcement with stated value >= **5%** of trailing four quarters'
  revenue. 5% is used, not row 84's 10%, because row 85 found no size effect and L1 contracts
  at EPC firms are often smaller. One event per company per 15 sessions; later ones inside the
  window are ignored.
- s0 = the last session whose close is before the announcement time.
- **Entry:** close of s0+1, the announcement day when the news comes in market hours.
- **Outcome:** stock minus its industry index (row 84's A control) over **5, 10 and 15
  sessions** from entry.

## 3. Members — one family, trials = 3

One member per horizon: L1-5, L1-10 and L1-15.

## 4. Test

- Statistic: mean outcome after charging **50 bps** (a round trip).
- p: one-sided, from a cluster bootstrap over calendar months of entry (10,000 reps, seed 1).
- Benjamini-Hochberg over the three horizons.
- **In-sample PASS** for a horizon needs all three of:
  - net mean >= **+2 pp** (the operator's bar);
  - BH q < 0.10;
  - positive in at least 3 of the 5 years 2018 (H2) - 2022.
- Reported but not judged:
  - entry at the next open;
  - entry one minute after the filing (2021-08 onward, from 1-min data);
  - the s0 to s0+1 move and the 20 sessions before the filing;
  - the median and the share beating the industry;
  - events by year;
  - the 10% size cut-off;
  - the share of L1 events followed by a row 84 award for the same company within 90 days.

## 5. Years

- **In-sample:** announcements 2018-07-01 to 2022-12-31.
- **Check:** announcements 2023-01-01 to 2025-12-31.
  - It runs once, with the rule unchanged, and only for horizons that pass in-sample.
  - PASS = net mean > 0 with p < 0.10, and positive in at least 2 of the 3 years.
  - No L1 returns in these years have been computed by any study.
  - A pass leads to a forward paper track. A fail closes the L1 idea for this definition.

## 6. What may change before the in-sample run, and what may not

May: fixing a bug in fetching or parsing, with the change logged here.

May not, after seeing a return: the 5% cut-off, the horizons, the entry, the control, the cost,
the pass bars or the years. Changing any of these sends the test back to exploration as a new
row.
