EXPLORATION — Weinstein Stage 2 (every name in it), 20-day forward return, entries at the open after the decision close
1904 decision days, 1203215 symbol-days, 15 dimensions, 92 buckets examined

Statistic: mean forward return of the names the pattern FIRED on inside a bucket,
minus the mean of every name in that same bucket on that same day.
'vol x' is how volatile the selected names are relative to their bucket — well
above 1.0 means the edge may be risk premium, not selection (LEDGER row 4).
'churn' is the share of the selected names replaced each holding period — what
actually trades; 'net' is the edge after 50 bps round trip on that churn.

'vs ctl' asks whether the rule does better or worse in a bucket than it would anyway.
A bucket of stocks is compared, day by day, with random groups of the SAME size drawn
from the names its dimension covered that day (a small bucket's top fifth is less
extreme, so comparing it with the whole universe would invent differences). A bucket
of days — year, breadth — is compared with the rule on all other days. The score is
that difference over its own standard error, from a block bootstrap
(1000 resamples, mean block 60 trading days,
so overlapping forward returns and slow-changing selections are not mistaken for
independent evidence). The same blocks are drawn for every bucket, so the threshold is
family-wise: with no real differences, the largest score of all 92 buckets exceeds 4.3
in only 5% of resamples. ◆ marks a real bucket beyond it — worth writing a mechanism
down for; still not a result.

Read the halves and the years too: an edge that lives in one of them is the shape
of LEDGER row 13's dead effect.
