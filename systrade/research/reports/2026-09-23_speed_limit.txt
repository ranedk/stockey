How to read it. The five live tracks hold 21 days and replace about two thirds of the
book each time, which is the 21-day row scaled by -churn: a cost in the low single
digits a year, against books that returned 13-19%. A swing book held a week is the
5-day row, and it starts every year that many points behind. Law 13's question is not
whether a fast signal predicts — it is whether it predicts by MORE than this table.

Two levers move these numbers, and neither is the signal: bigger positions (the DP
charge is flat, so it hurts small slices most) and auction fills (which pay no spread).
