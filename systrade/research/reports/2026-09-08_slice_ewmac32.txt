Statistic: mean forward return of the top-forecast fifth INSIDE a bucket, minus the
mean of every name in that same bucket on that same day. Each slice is its own
control, so the market's move and the slice's own drift both cancel.
'bottom' is the same for the WORST-forecast fifth: a real ordering loses there.
'vol x' is how volatile the selected names are relative to their bucket — well
above 1.0 means the edge may be risk premium, not selection (LEDGER row 4).

the shape of LEDGER row 13's dead effect. Take a MECHANISM from here, then test
it where this command has not looked.
