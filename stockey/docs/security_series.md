# Security Series


### 1. **Fully-paid equity shares / ETFs**

* **Typical Tags:**

`EQ` – normal rolling settlement

`BE / BZ` – trade-to-trade (delivery only, often for surveillance)

`SM` – SME platform (NSE Emerge)

`ST / SZ` – SME shares that move to trade-to-trade
* **What Trades Here:** Cash-market stocks and equity ETFs
* **Key Rule:**

* `EQ`: Intraday + delivery allowed
* `BE/BZ/ST/SZ`: Must take/give delivery, no intraday


---

### 2. **Block Deals**

* **Typical Tag:** `BL`
* **What Trades Here:** One-shot trades ≥ 5 lakh shares **or** ≥ ₹5 crore
* **Key Rule:** Settles gross; no netting with other trades

---

### 3. **Partly-paid equity shares**

* **Typical Tags:** `E@ / X@` (@ = 1–9 or A–Z)
* **What Trades Here:** Instalment-pay shares issued during an IPO or rights offer
* **Key Rule:** Delivery allowed; margin only on paid-up value

---

### 4. **Mutual-fund / ETF units (close-ended)**

* **Typical Tags:** `MF / ME`
* **What Trades Here:** Listed mutual fund schemes & some ETFs
* **Key Rule:** Trades like equity; no STT on buy

---

### 5. **Preference shares**

* **Typical Tags:**
* 
`P@` – non-convertible

`Q@` – fully-convertible

`O@ / F@` – trade-to-trade variants
* **What Trades Here:** Listed preference shares
* **Key Rule:** Mostly delivery-only

---

### 6. **Corporate & PSU bonds (NCDs)**

* **Typical Tags:** `N@, Y@, Z@, A@, B@, U@, M@` (@ = 0–9 or A–Z)
* **What Trades Here:** All rupee NCDs – fixed-coupon, zero-coupon, step-ups, floaters
* **Key Rule:** Fixed 5% / 10% margin (not VaR); settled T+2

---

### 7. **Convertible bonds**

* **Typical Tags:**

`D@` – fully convertible

`S@` – trade-to-trade variant
* **What Trades Here:** Debentures that later convert into equity
* **Key Rule:** Delivery only until conversion

---

### 8. **Warrants**

* **Typical Tags:**

`W@` – convertible

`K@` – trade-to-trade variant
* **What Trades Here:** Listed equity warrants
* **Key Rule:** Delivery only

---

### 9. **InvIT units**

* **Typical Tags:**

`IV` – normal

`ID` – trade-to-trade
* **What Trades Here:** Listed infrastructure-trust units
* **Key Rule:** Treated like equities for margin/settlement

---

### 10. **REIT units**

* **Typical Tags:**

`RR` – normal

`RT` – trade-to-trade
* **What Trades Here:** Real-estate-trust units
* **Key Rule:** Same as InvIT

---

### 11. **Government securities**

* **Typical Tags:**

`GS` – GoI dated G-Secs

`TB` – Treasury Bills

`SG` – State development loans
* **What Trades Here:** G-Secs traded in capital-market window
* **Key Rule:** T+1 settlement, 1% margin

---

### 12. **Sovereign-Gold Bonds**

* **Typical Tags:** `GB`, or numeric tags like `11, 12 … 19` (older tranches)
* **What Trades Here:** SGB units
* **Key Rule:** Margined like debt; no STT

---

### 13. **Rights entitlements**

* **Typical Tag:** `RE` (now trades under `BE`)
* **What Trades Here:** Temporary rights during a rights issue
* **Key Rule:** Cash-settled; expires before record date

---

### 14. **Buy-back window**

* **Typical Tag:** `BO`
* **What Trades Here:** Company buy-back via open market
* **Key Rule:** Gross settlement
